Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026 · Last updated July 24, 2026

Plain-language summary

Campaign Insights Connector accesses only the Google Ads data needed to provide reports, analysis, and explicitly approved account actions. The tool is private, runs locally, and does not sell or share Google Ads data.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Campaign Insights Connector handles information when its owner uses the tool with Google Ads accounts they own or administer.

Campaign Insights Connector is a private, local, single-user tool. It is not a public application, multi-tenant service, advertising agency platform, or data reseller.

2. Information accessed

When the user requests a report or analysis, the tool may access Google Ads account identifiers, account name, currency, time zone, campaign and ad group configuration, campaign status, budgets, targeting information, and performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value, cost per conversion, and return on ad spend.

The tool does not request Google Ads data from accounts that the authenticated user does not own or administer.

3. Purpose of access

Google Ads data is accessed solely to provide account reporting, campaign performance analysis, trend identification, and recommendations requested by the account owner.

Future management features may prepare and apply campaign changes only after displaying the proposed change and receiving the user's explicit approval. Data is not accessed for unrelated advertising, profiling, or audience-building purposes.

4. Storage and retention

Credentials and service-account keys are stored in private local configuration files on the user's device. They are not embedded in this website or published in source control.

Reports are requested on demand and processed for the user's analysis. The current version does not maintain a remote database of Google Ads data. Future local audit records may retain resource identifiers, timestamps, and before-and-after values for approved changes, but will not contain credentials.

5. Sharing and sale

Campaign Insights Connector does not sell, rent, license, or share Google Ads data with advertisers, data brokers, external customers, or other third parties.

The tool uses Google's official Google Ads API client library to communicate directly with Google Ads. Google processes information according to its own terms and privacy policies.

6. Security

Access is limited to the authenticated service account and the Google Ads permissions assigned by the account administrator. Local credential files are restricted to the device user.

The current production-testing version is read-only. Planned write operations will use previews, validation where available, explicit approval, limits on sensitive changes, and post-action verification.

7. User choices and access removal

The account owner can stop the tool's access at any time by removing the service account from Google Ads, disabling or deleting its credentials in Google Cloud, or deleting the local configuration.

Questions or requests about locally retained information may be sent to the contact address below.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the tool's functionality or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact trendytech355@gmail.com.
