Plain-language summary

Zombie Carnival: Monster Clash is supported by advertising. The game does not require players to create an account or provide direct contact information.

1. Information processed

The game and its advertising partners may process device and technical information, such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, operating-system version, app interactions, and diagnostic information.

2. Advertising

The game includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may process information to provide, measure, limit, and personalize ads according to your settings and Google’s policies. Where required, the game presents consent choices before requesting or using advertising-related data.

3. Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate advertising and app functionality, including Google Mobile Ads. Trendy Tech does not sell personal information.

4. Your choices

You can manage ad personalization through your device and Google account settings. You may also reset or limit your device advertising identifier through your device settings where those controls are available.

5. Children

The game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us so we can address the request.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact trendytech355@gmail.com.
