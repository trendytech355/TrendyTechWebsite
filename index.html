What it does

Built for focused, accountable campaign work.

The connector supports on-demand reporting today and a deliberately constrained management workflow in future releases.

01

Performance reporting

Review impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value, CPA, and ROAS across connected campaigns.

02

Account-level insights

Surface campaign trends and performance issues without replacing the controls and safeguards of Google Ads.

03

Controlled management

Planned updates are previewed, validated, and require explicit approval before any account change is applied.

Transparent by design

Why the tool requests Google Ads data

The connector requests access solely to retrieve account and campaign information needed to provide reporting, analysis, and user-approved account management. It does not request access for unrelated purposes.

Read the full privacy policy
Data accessed

Account identifiers, campaign settings, status information, and performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, cost, and conversions.

How it is used

To generate requested reports, explain performance, and prepare changes that remain subject to explicit user review and approval.

Who can use it

This is an internal, single-user tool. It is not offered as a public application, agency platform, or third-party service.

Privacy & control

Your account stays yours.

  • ✓Access is limited to accounts the user owns or administers.
  • ✓Credentials stay in a private local configuration on the user's device.
  • ✓The current production-testing version is read-only.
  • ✓Google Ads data is never sold or used for advertising profiles.
